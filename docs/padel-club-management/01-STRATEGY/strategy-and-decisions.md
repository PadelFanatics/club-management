# Strategy and Decisions

## Core offer

**Padel Club Management / Padel Club Scale** is a low-cost club-management SaaS with optional setup, marketing, AI, commerce, and content services.

The positioning is not a $2,000/month agency and not a full Playtomic replacement on day one. The fast launch sells an affordable operating platform and lets clubs buy implementation help when needed.

## Target

- Buyer: padel club owners/operators.
- Market: global English-speaking clubs from day one.
- Player account: free.
- Club trial: $1 for 30 days, card required.
- Club can upgrade at any time; it does not need to wait for trial expiry.
- Trial uses limited features and credits.

## Market position

Existing booking/club tools range from low-cost/free products to several hundred dollars per month. The platform differentiates through:

- affordable court-based plans;
- no launch booking commission;
- free player accounts;
- AI credit wallet;
- club-owned Kick/VOD content workflow;
- optional implementation training;
- Shopify/equipment commerce;
- future club and player design tools.

## $10,000 MRR direction

The first seven-day objective is not a guaranteed $10,000 MRR. The honest immediate target is a working offer, funnel, demo, prospect list, trials, setup sales, and a case-study club.

Longer term, $10,000 MRR can combine club subscriptions, credit-wallet margin, setup/support add-ons, marketing add-ons, player upgrades, commerce margin, supplier commissions, design services, and content services.

## Seven-day focus

1. Package the offer and pricing.
2. Build the trial/signup funnel.
3. Connect the credit-wallet concept.
4. Prepare a club demo from the existing app.
5. Build a global English-speaking club prospect list.
6. Start outreach and demos.
7. Onboard the first pilot clubs.

## Architecture principle

Use shared infrastructure and secure multi-tenancy where practical. Do not create separate bespoke codebases or inventories for every club unless a larger client pays for a dedicated environment.
