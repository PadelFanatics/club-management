# 30-Day Trial and Launch Plan

## Trial

- $1 activation.
- Card required.
- 30 days.
- 5,000 promotional credits.
- Upgrade at any time.
- Automatic conversion on day 30 unless cancelled.
- Clear billing reminders.

## Lifecycle

### Day 0
Create account, activate trial, grant credits, connect provider or choose managed credits, configure club.

### Day 1
Add courts, logo, colours, hours and administrator. Start training.

### Day 3
Invite players and configure booking/event settings.

### Day 5
Create first event and campaign.

### Day 7
Send early upgrade offer and show usage/progress.

### Day 14
Review players, campaigns, credits and setup completion. Offer launch setup.

### Day 21
Run first tournament, event or reactivation activity.

### Day 27
Show exact plan, price and conversion date.

### Day 30
Convert or cancel; retain the free account where appropriate.

## First seven days for the business

1. Confirm name, domains and master offer.
2. Install dependencies and run the application checks.
3. Audit Supabase schema and production gaps.
4. Define the credit wallet data model.
5. Create the club trial funnel.
6. Create the first club demo and onboarding videos.
7. Build a prospect list and begin outreach.

## First-club success criteria

- Club completes account setup.
- Club connects or funds AI usage.
- Club configures courts and events.
- Club invites players.
- Club runs one campaign.
- Club starts a Kick channel or agrees to the VOD plan.
- Club gives permission for a case study.
