# Shopify and mystore.gg Commerce — Vision

Build a central catalogue and supplier-direct commerce layer that can later support club-branded storefronts, tracked referrals and direct club ordering without holding inventory prematurely.

## Success measures

- Verified store access.
- Supplier, stock, margin and fulfilment data.
- Central catalogue.
- Club branding and attribution model.
- Refund, support and compliance process.

## Agent role

Research products, suppliers, margins and catalogue structure. Do not publish products, reactivate billing or place orders without verified access and approval.
