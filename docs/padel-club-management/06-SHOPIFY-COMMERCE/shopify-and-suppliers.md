# Shopify, mystore.gg and Supplier Commerce

## Master store

The existing Padel Fanatics Shopify store should be reactivated if its catalogue and settings remain available. It becomes the master product catalogue.

The intended commerce layer is:

```text
Padel Fanatics master store
+ mystore.gg
+ Scout.gg account infrastructure
+ club storefront templates
+ supplier-direct dropshipping
```

Authenticated access has not yet been inspected, so Shopify, mystore.gg and Scout.gg integration capabilities must be verified before live changes.

## Master catalogue

The master store controls:

- product data;
- supplier stock;
- prices and variants;
- images;
- shipping rules;
- returns;
- product status;
- supplier relationships.

## Club storefronts

Each club storefront gets:

- club logo and colours;
- club domain;
- club landing page;
- curated products;
- tournament collection;
- tracked referral/commission ID.

Prefer one central catalogue plus club configuration rather than duplicating every product into completely independent stores.

## Supplier-direct model

Begin with dropshipping and affiliate/commission relationships. Avoid holding inventory until bestsellers are proven.

Supplier data required:

- SKU;
- wholesale price;
- recommended retail price;
- stock feed/API;
- variants and images;
- shipping countries and delivery times;
- returns and warranty;
- dropship and packaging fees.

## Product categories

- rackets;
- balls;
- bags;
- grips;
- shoes and trainers;
- clothing;
- water and drinks;
- sunglasses;
- accessories;
- beginner bundles;
- tournament supplies;

## Revenue

- retail margin;
- affiliate commissions;
- club referral share;
- supplier commissions;
- featured placements;
- recurring replenishment orders;
- branded tournament kits;
- design and production fees.

All custom clothing and tournament kits must be paid before production.

## Rollout

1. Reactivate/audit Shopify.
2. Recover catalogue and supplier information.
3. Test one supplier fulfilment path.
4. Add a Padel Fanatics store link to the club platform.
5. Build one pilot club storefront.
6. Productise storefront cloning and domain setup.
