# Handover — Padel Club Management

## Objective

Create a global English-speaking padel-club SaaS and growth platform using the existing Padel Fanatics app, club-management code, credit-wallet concept, Kick VOD workflow, Shopify commerce and future design tools.

## Current decisions

- Target: padel club owners/operators.
- Market: global English-speaking clubs.
- Trial: $1 for 30 days, card required.
- Players: free accounts.
- Default plan: Small Club at $79/month.
- No launch booking or sales commission.
- AI: limited trial credits, managed credit wallet with transparent margin, optional BYOK.
- Video/storage: excluded; club owns Kick, GitHub, storage and processing costs.
- Shopify: master catalogue plus club storefront templates and supplier dropshipping.
- Design: later club/player apparel and tournament design toolkit.
