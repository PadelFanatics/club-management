# Padel Club Management — Vision Document

## North-star outcome

Build the operating system for independent padel clubs: a branded, affordable version of the power of a Scale-style marketing and operations suite, combined with the player, court, booking, match and tournament features that generic marketing platforms do not provide.

Working product direction: **ScalePadel / Padel Club Scale**.

## 30-day measurable commercial goal

Reach the first credible path toward **$10,000 monthly recurring revenue** within 30 days. This is a target, not a guaranteed result.

Illustrative subscription mix:

- 20 Growing clubs at $149/month = $2,980.
- 20 Pro clubs at $249/month = $4,980.
- 5 Multi-location clubs at $399/month = $1,995.
- 2 advanced CRM add-ons at $29/month = $58.

Illustrative total: **$10,013 MRR**.

The faster commercial route is not to finish every feature. It is to sell a narrow club-management wedge, onboard clubs manually where necessary, learn from usage and expand only what directly improves activation, retention or sales.

## Customer

Primary buyer: independent padel-club owner/operator or small club group.

Initial market: global English-speaking clubs.

Player access: free player accounts remain a growth channel.

## Launch promise

Give a padel club one place to run its club, grow its player base and market its events—without stitching together a booking tool, website builder, CRM, email system, tournament tool and content workflow.

## Day-7 product acceptance

A new club owner can:

1. Register and log in.
2. Create a club.
3. Become the club administrator.
4. Add courts and opening hours.
5. Invite players.
6. Create or receive a booking/enquiry.
7. Record a match and see a result/ranking.
8. Create one event or tournament.
9. Send or prepare one approved campaign.
10. See trial, plan and AI-credit status.

A second club cannot read or modify the first club’s data.

## Day-30 business acceptance

- A live public offer and signup/trial funnel.
- A working demo tenant.
- At least one pilot club using the flow.
- Qualified club pipeline in CRM.
- Trial and upgrade messaging.
- Verified billing path or clearly labelled manual payment handoff.
- Measured activation, player invites, bookings, matches, campaign usage and credit usage.
- A repeatable setup package documented as training.
- No secrets, payment data, customer data or provider keys in source control.

## Product layers

### Player layer

Scoring, match records, results, rankings, events, tournaments, training and community.

### Club operating layer

Club account, staff, locations, courts, schedules, bookings, player management, payments, events, tournaments and reports.

### Growth layer

Website, public club pages, forms, CRM, email campaigns, reactivation, calendar, offers, lead capture and analytics.

### AI and credit layer

Metered AI credits, usage history, spending limits, approval before consequential actions, managed provider keys and optional BYOK.

### Commerce and media layer

Club storefronts, supplier products, referrals, branded equipment and optional club-owned Kick/VOD workflows. Storage and video processing remain outside the base subscription.

## Agent operating model

Use multiple specialised agents, not one unrestricted agent. Start with three:

1. **Chief of Staff / ROB** — keeps the vision, priorities and decisions aligned.
2. **Build Lead** — works directly on the Padel codebase and reports tests.
3. **Revenue Operator** — prepares lead lists, outreach drafts, funnel tasks and follow-up reports.

Add agents only when a repeated bottleneck is proven:

- QA/Security reviewer.
- Supabase/data specialist.
- PageBuilder/Scale funnel builder.
- Content repurposing operator.
- Pilot onboarding operator.
- Commerce/affiliate operator.

Agents start in observe/report mode, then draft mode, then limited execution with human approval. No agent sends campaigns, changes bookings, issues credits, charges cards or changes production data without approval and verification.

## Working method from the transcript

1. Brain-dump goals, current assets, constraints and frustrations.
2. Ask the agent to reverse-prompt: identify the unknowns, opportunities and next actions.
3. Write the vision and acceptance tests before asking for implementation.
4. Give the agent measurable end states, not vague commands.
5. Start with a small number of agents.
6. Start each agent slowly: research → report → draft → supervised action → limited autonomy.
7. Speak directly to the specialist responsible for the work when possible.
8. Turn completed work into reusable content, training and proof.
9. Keep a decision log and update this vision when the commercial target changes.

## Priority order

1. Working club signup, auth, data isolation and club setup.
2. Courts, players, bookings and match/results loop.
3. Trial, plan display, billing handoff and credit limits.
4. Demo, pilot onboarding and one growth campaign.
5. Outreach, demos, setup packages and paid trials.
6. Retention and measured product improvements.
7. Advanced AI, commerce, VOD and design toolkit.

## Explicit non-goals for the first 30 days

- Building a full Playtomic replacement.
- Building every Scale-style application from scratch.
- Launching a universal supplier marketplace.
- Adding unlimited AI.
- Including storage/video in base subscriptions.
- Granting agents unsupervised access to money, accounts or customer communications.
