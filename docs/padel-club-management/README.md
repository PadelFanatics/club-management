# Padel Club Management

Working name: Padel Club Scale / Padel Club Management

## Purpose

Build an affordable global SaaS and growth platform for English-speaking padel club owners/operators. The platform combines club management, player accounts, bookings, rankings, events, tournaments, CRM/marketing, a credit wallet for metered AI usage, Kick VOD workflows, club storefronts, supplier dropshipping, and later a design toolkit.

## Current commercial decision

- Target buyer: padel club owners/operators.
- Initial market: global English-speaking clubs.
- Trial: $1 for 30 days; card required; upgrade available at any time.
- Players: free accounts remain available.
- No booking commission or sales commission during launch.
- Video hosting, VOD storage, and video processing are excluded.
- AI uses a limited trial allowance and then the club wallet or its own provider key.
- Default club plan: Small Club at $79/month.
