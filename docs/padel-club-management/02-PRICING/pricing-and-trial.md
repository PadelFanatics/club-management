# Pricing and Trial

## Club plans

| Plan | Best for | Price after trial |
|---|---|---:|
| Solo | 1–2 courts | $39/month |
| Small | 3–5 courts | $79/month |
| Growing | 6–10 courts | $149/month |
| Pro | 11–20 courts | $249/month |
| Multi-location | Multiple sites | From $399/month |

## Trial

- $1 for 30 days.
- Card required.
- Create an account for free, but the operating trial requires the $1 activation.
- Upgrade at any time.
- Convert automatically on day 30 unless cancelled.
- Trial has limits on courts, players, campaigns, automations, email and AI credits.
- No included storage, video hosting, VOD processing, SMS, or WhatsApp.
- No booking or sales commission during launch.

## Trial allowance

- One location.
- Up to 3 courts.
- Up to 100 players.
- One administrator.
- One campaign.
- One event.
- One tournament.
- One automation.
- 250 email sends.
- 5,000 promotional AI credits.

## Setup packages

- Quick Start: $49 one-time.
- Club Launch: $149 one-time.
- Growth Setup: $299 one-time.

Setup work should be recorded as training videos and converted into reusable onboarding modules.

## Conversion sequence

- Day 0: activate $1 trial, add card, grant credits.
- Day 7: early upgrade offer.
- Day 14: progress review and setup offer.
- Day 21: campaign and player-growth prompt.
- Day 27: billing reminder.
- Day 30: convert or cancel.

The day-7 offer can include launch pricing, free Quick Start setup, or additional credits. Clubs must be able to upgrade immediately rather than waiting for the trial to finish.
