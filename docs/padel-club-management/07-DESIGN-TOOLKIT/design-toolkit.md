# Club and Player Design Toolkit

## Future product

An AppBuilder-style design toolkit lets clubs and players create and order branded apparel and tournament assets.

## Club use cases

- tournament T-shirts;
- player shirts;
- staff uniforms;
- caps and hoodies;
- sponsor shirts;
- winners' shirts;
- team kits;
- event graphics;
- posters and certificates;
- court signage.

## Player use cases

- custom T-shirts;
- padel bags;
- hoodies;
- caps;
- team shirts;
- names and numbers;
- player avatars;
- limited-edition designs.

## Workflow

```text
Select template
↓
Add logo, colours, sponsors and names
↓
Generate proof
↓
Approve design
↓
Pay upfront
↓
Production and fulfilment
```
